Admissions

4 questions
How do I apply for admission?

Start with the admissions enquiry form on this website, or contact the college by phone or email. The admissions team can explain available places, entry requirements, documents and current fees.

What documents should a family prepare for an application?

The college will confirm the current document list. Families should generally be ready to share the learner’s birth certificate or identification, previous school reports, a transfer letter where applicable, and parent or guardian contact details.

Can families visit the school before applying?

Contact the admissions team to ask about a visit or conversation with the school. The team can advise on the best time and what to prepare for the discussion.

How do I ask about fees and available places?

Fees and availability can change, so the college handles these details directly. Contact the admissions team by phone or email for the current information.

Forms and stages

2 questions
Which forms does Goldphoenix Christian College offer?

Goldphoenix Christian College offers secondary education from Form 1 to Form 6.

What does Form 1–6 cover?

Form 1–6 provides secondary education with growing subject depth, study habits and preparation for the next step. Contact the college for the current subject list and options.

Day and boarding

2 questions
Does the college offer both day and boarding education?

Yes. The college advertises both day and boarding education. Please contact the school for current availability, boarding arrangements and related requirements.

What school-life arrangements are available?

The college offers day and boarding pathways. Contact the school to discuss timetables, transport, accommodation, supervision, meals, weekend routines and what learners should bring.

Results portal

2 questions
How can parents access student results?

Use the Student Results Portal link. The version on this website is a demonstration only and accepts the sample student number DEMO-001 with PIN 2026. Official access should be provided by the college through its secure student records system.

Is the results portal on this website live?

No. The portal on this website is a clearly marked demonstration. It does not contain real learner information and must not be used for admissions or academic decisions.
