Academics

Foundations first. Then depth, confidence and direction.

Goldphoenix Christian College offers primary learning from Grade 1–7 and secondary learning from Form 1–6, with purposeful teaching and support at every stage.

Academic overview

One school, two connected stages.

Quick answer

Primary school covers Grade 1–7 and focuses on literacy, numeracy and strong foundations. Secondary school covers Form 1–6 and builds subject depth, independent study and preparation for the next step.

Primary · Grade 1–7

Building the confidence to learn.

The primary years are where curiosity, reading, number sense and good learning habits take root. Learners are guided with clear routines, encouragement and age-appropriate challenge.

The learning approach

Teaching that builds understanding, not just coverage.

Good learning is purposeful, relational and responsive. These four commitments shape the classroom experience.

01

Strong foundations

Clear explanations, guided practice and the confidence to ask good questions.

02

Growing independence

Study habits, organisation and self-belief that become more important each year.

03

Known learners

Teachers who notice progress, offer support and encourage the next step.

04

Purposeful feedback

Assessment used to show what is working and what needs attention next.

Subject areas

A broad school experience.

Subject offerings can change by year and form. Contact the college for the current subject list, options and assessment arrangements.

Ask about subjects
  • Languages and literacy
  • Mathematics and numeracy
  • Sciences
  • Humanities and social studies
  • Religious and moral education
  • Business and practical subjects
  • Arts and creativity
  • Sport and physical education

Assessment with a purpose.

Assessment should help learners and families understand progress, not simply produce a mark. The college can explain current reporting and assessment arrangements.

Support around the learner.

Teachers, pastoral care and families working together give learners the best chance to succeed. Ask the college about the support available for your child.

Preparation for the next step.

From primary into secondary and beyond, the goal is a learner who is ready to make thoughtful choices with confidence.
