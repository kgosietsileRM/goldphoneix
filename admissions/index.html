The process

Four straightforward steps.

The college will guide your family through its current application process. Start with an enquiry and the admissions team will explain what applies to your child.

  1. 01

    Start an enquiry

    Share your child’s details, the grade or form you are considering, and your preferred pathway.

  2. 02

    Talk with the school

    The admissions team can explain available places, requirements, fees and boarding arrangements.

  3. 03

    Prepare and apply

    Complete the school’s current application requirements and provide the requested documents.

  4. 04

    Confirm and enrol

    Once placement is confirmed, the college guides your family through the next steps.

What to prepare

Documents to have ready.

The college will confirm the current document list. Preparing the essentials early helps the conversation move forward.

  • Completed admissions enquiry and application
  • Learner’s birth certificate or identification
  • Previous school reports or academic records
  • Transfer letter where applicable
  • Parent or guardian contact details
  • Boarding requirements discussed with the school

Fees and availability

Ask for the current information.

Fees, places and boarding arrangements can change. The college handles these details directly, so families receive accurate, current information for their learner.

Admissions questions

A few things families often ask.

If your question is not here, the full FAQ page covers grades, day and boarding, the results portal and contact details.

Read all FAQs
How do I apply for admission?

Start with the admissions enquiry form on this website, or contact the college by phone or email. The admissions team can explain available places, entry requirements, documents and current fees.

What documents should a family prepare for an application?

The college will confirm the current document list. Families should generally be ready to share the learner’s birth certificate or identification, previous school reports, a transfer letter where applicable, and parent or guardian contact details.

Can families visit the school before applying?

Contact the admissions team to ask about a visit or conversation with the school. The team can advise on the best time and what to prepare for the discussion.

How do I ask about fees and available places?

Fees and availability can change, so the college handles these details directly. Contact the admissions team by phone or email for the current information.
