Our school

A Christian learning community in the heart of Gweru.

Goldphoenix Christian College is a private Christian boarding and day high school in Daylesford, Gweru. Our motto is Excellence Beyond Expectations, from Form 1 to Form 6.

Our purpose

Excellence beyond expectations is a promise about the whole learner.

Academic progress matters. So does the kind of person a learner becomes. Goldphoenix Christian College brings these together through a Christian ethos, clear expectations and a community that values each learner.

The college serves families in Gweru and beyond, with day and boarding pathways for Form 1–6 learners. Families are encouraged to speak directly with the school about current arrangements, available places and the best fit for their child.
